### Cool Science
The science behind alien life is very cool, particularly when considering the astrobiology involved in producing such life. But one of the great complexities is how you can even detect life on another planet. One method involves looking at the age of a solar system's sun to gauge how old the planets are. If the sun is older than ours, it suggests its planets are too, which may provide the chance for life to have developed. But there are other ways of detecting life, such as <a href="https://www.universetoday.com/articles/older-alien-worlds-might-still-be-stuck-in-the-slime-age" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">measuring the relative level of carbon</a>. This study touches on how carbon on Earth rose when humans first entered the scene, and how this could be a rough indicator of intelligent life. Although there are several other factors that should be considered (including tidal movements and sun exposure), it's always interesting learning how "life" can be inferred on planets lightyears from our own, particularly as we may never get the chance to visit and test whether our hypotheses held true.
