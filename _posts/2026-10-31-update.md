### Cool Science
When moderately-sized stars (like our Sun) die, they expand outwards as they lose their gravitational hold on gas. This itself can destroy the inner planets that orbit it, creating a debris disk consisting of the planets that once basked in its sunlight. Yet what's even more interesting is that astronomers theorise <a href="https://www.universetoday.com/articles/dead-planets-orbiting-a-dead-star" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">it may be possible for planets to form from this debris around the dead star</a>. The study identified two dead planets orbiting a dead star. Although this doesn't prove these planets were formed from the debris, it's interesting to consider the possibilities of such an event. It's amazing how much can be inferred from something so distant from our own planet and Sun, let alone what else may be possible if you outlived a universe to experience it.
