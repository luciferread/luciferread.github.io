What's an ARC, exactly? ▸

An ARC (Advance Reader Copy) is a free copy of the book sent out before its public release, in exchange for an honest review once it launches — no editing, no payment, just an early read and a real opinion.

Since honesty is the whole point, this works best with readers who don't already know me personally. If you know someone who'd genuinely enjoy The Radiants, feel free to pass this page along — that's exactly how it's meant to spread.
