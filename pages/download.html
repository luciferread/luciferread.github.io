How to open your book

📱 iPhone & iPad ▸
  1. Tap Download Free Sample above.
  2. When prompted, tap Open in Books — it opens Apple Books immediately.
  3. No prompt? Open the Files app → Downloads → tap the file → Share → Copy to Books.
🤖 Android ▸
  1. Install Google Play Books (free) if you don't have it.
  2. Tap Download Free Sample above and save the file.
  3. Open Google Play Books → tap + → Upload from device → select the file.
  4. Or open your Downloads folder, tap the file, and choose Open with Play Books.
📖 Kindle ▸
  1. Download the file using the button above.
  2. Visit amazon.com/sendtokindle in any browser.
  3. Sign in to Amazon, then drag or upload the downloaded file.
  4. Select your Kindle from the list — the book appears in your library within seconds.
💻 Desktop & Laptop ▸
  1. Click Download Free Sample — the file saves to your Downloads folder.
  2. Open it with your preferred reader:
    • Mac — double-click to open in Apple Books (built in)
    • Windows / Linux — Calibre (free)
    • Any browser — Thorium Reader (free)
📚 Kobo ▸
  1. Download the file using the button above.
  2. Connect your Kobo to your computer with a USB cable.
  3. Your Kobo appears as a drive — drag the file into the Books folder.
  4. Eject the Kobo — the book appears in your library automatically.
